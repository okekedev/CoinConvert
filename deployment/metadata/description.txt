Point your camera at any price and see it in your home currency instantly. Tagwise is the travel currency converter that reads price tags, menus and receipts for you, with no typing.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open Tagwise and point your camera at a price
2. The converted amount appears instantly
3. Several prices in view? Tap the one you want
